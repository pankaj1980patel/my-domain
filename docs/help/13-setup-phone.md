On the phone, **Settings → Set up this phone** lists every optional permission and setting my-domain can use, what each is for, and whether it is on. Everything here is optional: turn on only what you want to use.

## my-domain never turns anything on itself

**Turn on…** opens Android's own screen or permission question, and you decide there. Come back to the app and the list updates.

## What each item is for

- **Notifications**: incoming files, rings, notifications shared from your computers, lost mode.
- **Notification access**: this phone's notifications on your computers, verification codes, and what's playing.
- **Accessibility helper**: lets your computers switch radios, press Back, Home and Lock, and control the screen while you share it.
- **Unrestricted battery use**: keeps my-domain reachable in the background. Pick my-domain and choose "Don't optimise".
- **Nearby devices (Bluetooth)**: reach your computers over Bluetooth when you're not on the same network.
- **Share this phone's location** and **Location permission**: find my phone, and listing Wi-Fi networks on your computers.
- **Photos and videos**: browse and back up photos from a computer.
- **Microphone**: needed by Android to play this phone's audio on a computer (Android 10 and later). Never used for calls or to record you.
- **Modify system settings**: brightness, screen timeout and auto-rotate from a computer.
- **Full-screen notifications**: lost mode's message over the lock screen.
- **Shizuku**: switch Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, mobile data and airplane mode with no tap on the phone. Needs the separate Shizuku app, started again after each restart.
- **Widget** and **Quick Settings tiles**: see [Widget and Quick Settings tiles](help:widget-tiles).

Some items say **Not needed on this Android version**: that version already allows it.
