Notes is a small notepad that's the same on all your devices: quick notes, lists, things to remember. Write one on your computer and it's on your phone too. Notes go straight between your devices, encrypted like everything else: nothing is stored on a server or in a cloud.

## Write a note

- **On the computer:** choose **Notes** in the sidebar, then **New note**. Or press **Cmd+K** (**Ctrl+K** on Windows and Linux) and type **New note**; type **Notes:** and a title to open one.
- **On the phone:** tap **Notes** in the bottom bar, then **New note**. Or, without opening the app first: tap **+** on the Notes widget, the **New note** Quick Settings tile, or touch and hold the app icon and choose **New note**.

A note saves itself a moment after you stop typing. There's no Save button.

- **Pin** keeps a note at the top of the list. The others follow, most recently changed first.
- **Colour** marks a note so you can spot it in the list.
- **Search** looks in titles and text. Every word you type has to be in the note, in any order. Notes also come up when you search everything (the command palette or the Search screen on the computer, the search field on the phone’s home screen); picking one opens it here.

## On the phone's home screen

The **Notes** widget shows your pinned notes, then the latest ones, with their first line and a bell on those with a reminder to come. Tap one to open it, or **+** for a new note. It follows your changes on every device and works while the app isn't running. See [Widget and Quick Settings tiles](help:widget-tiles) to add it.

## Checklists

Turn on **Checklist** and lines that start with `[ ] ` show as boxes you can tick; a ticked one starts with `[x] `. It's still plain text underneath: **Edit as text** shows it as typed, and other devices see the same text.

## Reminders

A note can remind you at a time. Open it and choose **Remind me**: pick **Later today**, **Tomorrow** (9:00) or **Next week** (Monday 9:00), or a date and time of your own. A bell marks notes with a reminder in the list, and **Upcoming** shows only those still to come, soonest first.

At that time **every device of yours that has the note reminds you**, whichever is on: a notification with the note's title and its first line.

- **Done** stops it, on all your devices: once they sync, the reminder goes from the others too.
- **Snooze** reminds you again in **10 min**, in **1 hour**, or **tomorrow at 9:00**, on all your devices.
- **On the phone**, the notification has **Done**, **Snooze 10 min** and **Snooze 1 hour**; tap it to open the note, which also has **Tomorrow 9:00**. Reminders still come after the phone restarts. Android may let this app set only inexact alarms (Android 12 and later can ask you); then a reminder can come a few minutes late, which the note says, with **Allow exact alarms** to change it in Android's settings. The app never turns that on by itself.
- **On the computer**, a reminder shows as a notification and in the my-domain window, with **Done**, **Open** and **Snooze** (computer notifications can't carry buttons everywhere). It works while my-domain is running; one that came due while it was closed or the computer was asleep shows once when it's back, if it's less than a day late. A notification already shown on the computer stays in the system's list after Done elsewhere; the one in the window goes.

A device with an older app keeps your notes but not their reminders, and it never removes them: editing a note there keeps its reminder on your other devices.

## When your devices sync

Right away while your devices are connected to each other. A device that's off or out of reach gets the changes the next time it connects; nothing wakes a sleeping phone just for a note. Only your own devices get your notes, and only ones with an app new enough to have Notes.

## Deleting

**Delete** removes the note from all your devices. **Undo** (right after) brings it back. A deleted note is remembered for 30 days so the delete reaches devices that were away; a device that's been away longer than that and still has the note brings it back.

## Conflicts

If the same note is changed on two devices before they hear of each other's change, no text is lost: the one changed last keeps the note, and the other text is saved as a separate note titled **Title (conflict from <device>)**, marked **Conflict copy**, on every device. Open both, keep what you want, delete the other.

## Limits

Up to 2,000 notes, each up to 64 KB of text, and 16 MB in all.
