Notes is a small notepad that's the same on all your devices: quick notes, lists, things to remember. Write one on your computer and it's on your phone too. Notes go straight between your devices, encrypted like everything else: nothing is stored on a server or in a cloud.

## Write a note

- **On the computer:** choose **Notes** in the sidebar, then **New note**. Or press **Cmd+K** (**Ctrl+K** on Windows and Linux) and type **New note**; type **Notes:** and a title to open one.
- **On the phone:** tap **Notes** in the bottom bar, then **New note**.

A note saves itself a moment after you stop typing. There's no Save button.

- **Pin** keeps a note at the top of the list. The others follow, most recently changed first.
- **Colour** marks a note so you can spot it in the list.
- **Search** looks in titles and text. Every word you type has to be in the note, in any order.

## Checklists

Turn on **Checklist** and lines that start with `[ ] ` show as boxes you can tick; a ticked one starts with `[x] `. It's still plain text underneath: **Edit as text** shows it as typed, and other devices see the same text.

## When your devices sync

Right away while your devices are connected to each other. A device that's off or out of reach gets the changes the next time it connects; nothing wakes a sleeping phone just for a note. Only your own devices get your notes, and only ones with an app new enough to have Notes.

## Deleting

**Delete** removes the note from all your devices. **Undo** (right after) brings it back. A deleted note is remembered for 30 days so the delete reaches devices that were away; a device that's been away longer than that and still has the note brings it back.

## Conflicts

If the same note is changed on two devices before they hear of each other's change, no text is lost: the one changed last keeps the note, and the other text is saved as a separate note titled **Title (conflict from <device>)**, marked **Conflict copy**, on every device. Open both, keep what you want, delete the other.

## Limits

Up to 2,000 notes, each up to 64 KB of text, and 16 MB in all.
