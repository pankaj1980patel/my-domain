Activity is one timeline of what happened between your devices: files sent and received, clipboard shared, messages, calls, notifications, devices connecting and going away, notes changed, links saved to read later, timers and more. It's grouped by day, newest first.

## Open it

- **On the computer:** choose **Activity** in the sidebar (or press **Cmd+K**, **Ctrl+K** on Windows and Linux, and type **Activity**).
- **On the phone:** tap **Activity** at the bottom of the screen.

Each row says what happened ("Sent report.pdf to Pixel"), with the device, the size and how it went (for example **via Wi-Fi Direct**) and the time. Tap a row to open it where it lives: a file in **Transfers**, a message or clip on its device's page, a note in **Notes**, a link in **Read later**, a timer in **Timers**, or the device itself. On the computer, **Show in folder** next to a file you sent or received shows it in Finder, Explorer or your file manager.

## Find something

- Type in the search box: every word must be in the row (ignoring capitals and accents), or in its device's name.
- Pick a **Kind** (Files, Clipboard, Messages, Calls, Notifications, Connections, Other) or a **Device** to see only those.
- **Load more** at the bottom goes further back.

## Choose what shows

**Choose what shows** lists the kinds; untick the ones you don't want in Activity. They're only hidden from this list: nothing is deleted.

## Clear activity

**Clear activity** forgets the things kept only for Activity (devices connecting and going away, calls, rings, screens and sounds shared, wake-ups, scheduled actions) and hides older rows. Your transfers, clipboard history, messages and notifications are **not** deleted: they stay in their own lists, and each of those has its own **Clear**. **Show earlier** brings the hidden rows back (those still kept).

## What's in it, and privacy

Activity is built on this device from what it already keeps; nothing extra is sent anywhere or synced. Each device shows what it saw itself, so your computer and your phone may list different things.

Passwords and other copies a password manager marks as private are never kept in the clipboard history, so they never show in Activity either. A clip shows only its first line.
