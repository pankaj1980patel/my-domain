## This device card

A device's page shows its battery, storage, memory and how long it has been on. Computers also show how busy their processor is (Android doesn't let apps read that).

## Battery graph

The last 24 hours of battery for each of your other devices, with charging shaded. Hover (or touch) to see the time and level. When there is enough data it estimates "About 6 h left at this rate" or "Full in about 40 min". The history is kept for 48 hours on the device that shows it.

## Battery alerts

Your devices can tell the others once when the battery drops below a level (15% unless you change it) and when it has charged to 80%, 90% or 100%. Turn them on or off in Settings.

## Other device controls

Depending on the device, its page can also turn the phone's flashlight on, change volume or ringer mode, keep a computer awake for a while, or wake a sleeping computer on the same network.
