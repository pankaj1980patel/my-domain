## Ring

**Ring** makes the other device play a sound so you can find it.

- A phone rings at full alarm volume, even on silent or vibrate.
- A computer shows a banner and plays a sound. Click anywhere on it to stop.

If the device isn't connected, the ring waits and goes as soon as it is.

## Lock

- **Lock a computer** from your phone: from its page, the widget or the **Lock computer** Quick Settings tile.
- **Lock this computer when my phone goes away** (computer Settings, off by default): when none of your phones has been connected for the time you pick, the computer locks once and tells you.
- **Lock a phone** from a computer: this uses the phone's Accessibility helper, so it has to be turned on on the phone.

## Lost mode

Lost mode is for a phone you can't find. Start it from the phone's page on a computer and type a message and a number to call.

- The phone shows your message over the lock screen, without unlocking it, with a **Call owner** button that opens the dialer with your number.
- It rings once.
- It shares its location only if location sharing was already turned on there.
- It stays on after a restart.
- Stop it from the computer, or with **I found it** on the phone after unlocking it.

Lost mode works best with **Full-screen notifications** allowed on the phone (see [Set up this phone](help:setup-phone)).

## What it can't do

my-domain can't erase a phone remotely, and it can't reach a phone that is switched off or has no connection at all. Anything you send waits until it is back.
