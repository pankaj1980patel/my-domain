**Read later** is a list of links you want to come back to, the same on all your devices. Save an article on your phone and it's waiting on your computer, or the other way round. Links go straight between your devices, encrypted like everything else: nothing is stored on a server or in a cloud.

Nothing is fetched from the web. A link shows the title it was shared with (when the app you shared from gave one), otherwise its site's name, such as **example.com**. Only **http** and **https** links can be saved.

## Save a link

- **On the phone:** in any app, tap **Share** and choose **Read later** (my-domain's own choice in the share sheet, next to sending to a device). Or open **Read later** in the app and paste a link.
- **On the computer:** choose **Read later** in the sidebar and paste a link (a title is optional), or click **Save copied link** to save the link you just copied. You can also press **Cmd+K** (**Ctrl+K** on Windows and Linux) and type **Save copied link**, or choose **Save copied link to Read later** from the menu bar or tray icon.

Saving a link that's already in the list moves it back to the top, unread. A copy marked private by a password manager is never saved (see [Privacy](help:privacy)).

## Read, mark and delete

- **Open** a link (click or tap it) to read it in your browser. It's marked read on all your devices.
- **Mark read** and **Mark unread** change it by hand.
- **Unread** shows what's still to read, newest first; **All** shows the read ones too.
- **Delete** removes it from all your devices. **Undo** (right after) brings it back.
- **Search** looks in titles, sites and the links themselves. Saved links also come up when you search everything (the command palette or the Search screen on the computer, the search field on the phone).

On the computer, the command palette lists each unread link as **Read later: <title>**; choose one to open it.

## When your devices sync

Right away while your devices are connected to each other. A device that's off or out of reach gets the changes the next time it connects; nothing wakes a sleeping phone just for a link. Only your own devices get your list, and only ones with an app new enough to have Read later.

If a link is changed on two devices before they hear of each other (marked read on one, deleted on the other), the change made last wins on every device. A deleted link is remembered for 30 days so the delete reaches devices that were away.

## Limits

Up to 5,000 links, each up to 4 KB long, with titles up to 300 characters.
