## Home-screen widget

Shows your computer on the phone's home screen: its name, whether it is connected, its battery, and **Send clipboard**, **Lock** and **Ring** buttons.

To add it: touch and hold an empty spot on the home screen, choose **Widgets**, then my-domain. Or use **Add to home screen** in Set up this phone.

When my-domain isn't running, the widget says so. Open the app to connect.

## Notes widget

A second widget shows your [notes](help:notes): pinned ones first, then the most recently changed, each with its title and first line, and a bell on notes with a reminder still to come. It shows as many as fit: make it taller for more (up to five).

- Tap a note to open it in my-domain.
- Tap **+** to start a new note.
- Tap **Notes** at the top to open the list.

It changes as soon as your notes do, here or on your other devices, and it still shows them when my-domain isn't running. Until the app has been opened once, it asks you to open it. Add it the same way as the other widget: touch and hold the home screen, choose **Widgets**, then my-domain's **Notes**.

## Quick Settings tiles

Three tiles for Android's Quick Settings panel: **Send clipboard**, **Lock computer** and **New note** (opens my-domain straight into a new note).

To add them: swipe down twice from the top of the screen, tap the pencil (Edit), and drag the tiles in. Android doesn't tell apps whether you've added them, so the Set up this phone checklist can't show whether they're on.

## New note from the app icon

Touch and hold my-domain's icon and choose **New note** to start one straight away. You can also drag it onto the home screen as its own shortcut.

## Which computer

The computer widget and the Send clipboard and Lock tiles act on one computer: one that can be locked, preferring one that is connected now. my-domain remembers it.
