Print from any app on your phone on a printer connected to one of your computers: the computer shows up in Android's print dialog with its printers, and the document goes to it as a PDF over the usual link.

## Turn it on

1. **On the computer:** Settings → **Print from your phone** → **Let my phone print here** (on by default). The tile lists the printers your phone will see.
2. **On the phone:** the first time, turn on the **my-domain** print service in Android's settings (**Settings → Connected devices → Printing**, or the button in my-domain's Settings under **Print from your phone**).

## Print

In any app, choose **Print** (or **Share → Print**), then pick a printer under **my-domain**: each is named after the printer and shows which computer it's on. Choose copies, colour or black and white, two-sided, paper size, orientation and pages as usual, and tap the print button.

Android's print notification follows the job: sending, waiting for the computer, printing, then done; or failed, with why (the printer refused it, it's no longer there, printing was turned off, or someone said no on the computer).

## Ask before printing

Turn on **Ask before printing** on the computer and each job waits there until someone clicks **Print**. **Don't print**, or no answer within two minutes, cancels it and the phone says so.

## Good to know

- **Mac and Linux** print through CUPS, the printing system they already use: any printer that works from the computer works from the phone.
- **Windows** can't print a PDF by itself (it needs a PDF app, and none can be counted on), so a Windows PC doesn't offer printing to your phone. Its Settings tile says so.
- The PDF goes to the computer like any other file (big documents are fine, and the transfer resumes if the link drops), but it's kept out of your downloads: it's deleted as soon as the printer has it, or the job fails.
- Only your own devices can print, and only computers that turned it on are listed. A computer whose app is too old to print never gets a print job.
- Printed documents show in the computer's **Activity** timeline.
