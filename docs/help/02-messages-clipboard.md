## Messages

Each device's page has a chat. Messages go straight to that device, end-to-end encrypted.

### Message history

Your chats are kept on each device, so they're still there after a restart: the newest 2,000 messages per device (20,000 in all), up to 64 KB each. The newest 100 show first; scroll up (or tap **Show older messages**) for older ones. On the computer, search finds saved messages too.

The history stays on that device only. It's never synced to your other devices or the server.

- **Clear** on a chat forgets that chat on this device, saved messages included.
- Turn **Keep message history** off in Settings to stop saving and forget everything kept.

## Clipboard

- **Send clipboard** puts what you copied onto the other device's clipboard, ready to paste.
- **Get clipboard** fetches what the other device has copied (only while it is connected).
- **Clipboard sync** (in Settings) sends what you copy to your other devices automatically.
- On the phone, Android only lets apps read the clipboard while they are on screen. The **Send clipboard** Quick Settings tile and widget button open an invisible screen for a moment to do it.
- On the computer, you can also give "send my clipboard to my phone" a keyboard shortcut. See [Automations and shortcuts](help:automations).

Copied pictures can be sent too.

## Clipboard history

Text sent or received as clipboard is kept on each device: the newest 200 clips, up to 10 KB each. On a device's page you can search it, **Copy** a clip again or **Send again**. On the computer the tray menu also has **Recent clips**.

Copied pictures sent or received are kept too: the newest 50, and no more than 100 MB of them in all (whichever comes first; the oldest go). Each shows a small thumbnail, its size in pixels and on disk, when, and which way it went. You can:

- **Copy** it onto this device's clipboard again, to paste in any app.
- **Send again** to that device's clipboard.
- **Save as…** (computer: pick where) or **Save** (phone: into Pictures/my-domain; Android 10 or later).
- **Open** it larger, or tap the thumbnail.

Search finds a picture by "picture" or "image", and by device or time (not by what's in it). Clearing a device's clips, or turning the history off, deletes the kept pictures too.

It stays on that device only. Turn **Keep a clipboard history** off in Settings to stop it and forget what was kept.

## Send text and links

You can also send a link to open in the other device's browser (web links only), or text to show there.

## Verification codes

If you turn it on in the phone's Settings, one-time codes that arrive in notifications are put on your computer's clipboard. It is off by default and needs notification access on the phone.
